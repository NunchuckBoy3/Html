HELLO time to see food from New jersey
  • Pork Roll: This pork-based meat product, often called Taylor Ham in North Jersey, is a staple of New Jersey diners, especially when served in a breakfast sandwich with eggs and cheese.
  • Salt Water Taffy: A sweet treat originating from Atlantic City, this chewy candy is a popular souvenir and summer staple.
  • Tomato Pie: A regional specialty, especially in Trenton and South Orange, tomato pie features a thin crust topped with cheese and then a generous layer of sweet tomato sauce.
